Week 5: the real-life offboarding flow

An employee's last day arrives; the agent starts by itself, collects everything about the person, asks for approval, then works the case every day (collecting, learning, asking owners) until it can tell HR the account is ready to delete.

Status
active · replanned with you on 2026-10-18 (Q29–Q32); PR 5.0–5.5c merged, 5.6 in review · updated 2026-10-18
Delivers
A simulated company that reacts, a vector DB, a case workflow with a daily agentic loop, Slack for HR and approvers
Done when
The recorded demo goes from last day to "ready for delete" with every gate 0 and every action approved

Weeks 1–4 built one investigation and one approval. Real offboarding is a case that lasts weeks: the last day is known in advance, blockers appear (a job still uses the person's key, a team still needs a role), owners have to act, and only when everything is resolved should anyone delete. Week 5 builds that case as an agentic process. The world is simulated but realistic, and every system sits behind an interface, so a real Okta, Jira, Vault or AWS can replace it later (week 6 option).

Unchanged: the investigator's guardrails, the executor (disable first, undo, idempotency), the hash-chained audit log, the bench gates, approval on Slack buttons by allow-listed people. The chat from PR 5.1 stays and gets case tools.

The demo

Dana Levi resigned two weeks ago; HR recorded her last day. Each ▶ Next day click moves the simulated calendar one day.

day 0 ⏰ trigger: Dana's last day. #hr-offboarding: "Dana Levi (Data Engineer) leaves today. Collecting her accounts and activity…"

agent investigates on its own: HR record, 12 accounts in the identity provider, 3 vault secrets (metadata only), app owners, Jira, logs, the vector DB. Card: 3 blockers: the nightly job uses her key (owner Mia Kim), she is the only Snowflake admin, ticket FIN-221 open · [Approve offboarding] [Decline]

HR [Approve offboarding] → her accounts are disabled (reversible); the case is on hold

day 1 🟡 On hold: the nightly job tried her key at 02:00. Asked Mia Kim to move it; commented on FIN-221.

day 3 Mia Kim: "moved the job to svc-reports". Indexed. Blocker resolved; 2 left.

you @agent why is Dana still on hold?

bot Two blockers: she is the only Snowflake admin (asked Omer Cohen, no answer yet), FIN-221 is open.Guardrails: Dana Levi: not yet. 2 blockers open; 11 days left of the 14-day quiet period.

day 14 🟢 Ready for delete: no use for 14 days, every owner confirmed, no open tickets. → HR alert · [Approve delete]

HR [Approve delete] → deleted, audit log, case closed

"Ready" is the model's proposal; code checks it (no use for 14 days, every blocker resolved with evidence, every owner confirmed, no open ticket by a person). If a check fails, the case stays on hold and the reason is shown.

Decisions

QuestionDecision
What starts a case?The last day recorded in HR. A daily trigger asks "whose last day is today?"; nobody clicks Start
Which model?OpenRouter Gemma 4 for the investigation, the daily assessment and the chat; the rule model offline and in CI
What may the agent do alone?Read every system, add to the vector DB, update the case, ask owners in Slack, open or comment on Jira tickets, alert HR. Disable and delete need a person's click
Who decides "ready for delete"?The model proposes it; code checks it (14 days without use, every blocker resolved with evidence, every owner confirmed, no open ticket by a person)
Secrets?The vault stores values encrypted; no tool returns a value, only name, owner, last rotated, who uses it
Keycloak, AWS?Week 6 option: real adapters behind the same interfaces

Questions

All answered on 2026-10-18.

Q25decided

How long does the bot remember a Slack thread?

Decided: 7 days per thread; nothing from a thread reaches an investigation.

Q26decided

Can someone start a case for a person HR has not reported?

Decided: only with a button that approvers can click; the chat stays read-only.

Q29decided

Real systems or a simulation?

Decided: a rich simulation: HR system, identity provider, credential vault, app catalog, Jira, logs, and people who answer the agent; each behind an interface.

Q30decided

What may the agent do on its own?

Decided: safe actions only: read, index, update the case, ask owners, open or comment on tickets, alert HR. Disable and delete always need a click.

Q31decided

How does time move in the demo?

Decided: a ▶ Next day button (Slack and make next-day); nothing moves until you click. Production runs on the calendar, each morning.

Q33decided

How do simulated people appear in Slack, without emails or accounts?

Decided: the bot speaks as them in #owners (a name and icon on each message, scope chat:write.customize). Real people later: a DM found by email (users.lookupByEmail), behind the same Outreach interface.

Q34decided

Who answers for them?

Decided: you, by button or in your own words; if nobody answers by the next day, their AI stand-in answers, with the outcome the simulation gives (the model only words it). CIA_OUTREACH_PERSONA=off turns the stand-ins off.

Q35default

Is the leaver's manager asked?

Default: yes, on the last day, in code: "does your team still need anything of theirs?" A delete waits for the answer. Easy to change.

Q36default

What if nobody answers?

Default: a reminder after 2 days; after 4 days the person's manager is told, and the case thread says so. Easy to change.

Q32decided

Which vector DB?

Decided: pgvector (Postgres in Docker) behind a DocIndex interface; embeddings from Ollama nomic-embed-text; a hashing embedder offline and in CI.

Risks

RiskMitigation
The model declares "ready" too earlyCode checks every ready proposal; a test with a model that always says ready deletes nothing
The agent spams ownersAt most one ask per owner per blocker every 3 days, in code
A ticket or reply carries instructionsTool results are data; actions come only from the allow-listed safe tools; destructive tools do not exist for the model
A secret leaks into a prompt or SlackNo tool returns a value; a test scans prompts and messages for the vault's values
The simulation looks scriptedOwners answer after a random delay from a seed; blockers differ per person; the demo uses the real model
Docker or Ollama is downSearch fails as a tool error, never "no docs"; CI uses the in-memory index and hashing embedder
